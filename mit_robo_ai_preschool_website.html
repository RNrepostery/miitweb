About MIT Robo AI Preschool

MIT Robo AI Preschool is a pioneering early learning initiative focused on nurturing young minds through AI, Robotics, Coding and experiential learning. We believe in making early education fun, meaningful and future-ready.

Our Director

Founder & Director

"With a vision to make future skills accessible from the earliest years, Mr. Sanjay Singh founded MIT Robo AI Preschool to bring innovation, creativity and technology into early childhood education. His mission is to empower every child with 21st century skills through joyful and meaningful learning."
